LONG DESCRIPTION
──────────────────────────────────────
Finance Tracker is a simple, private and powerful expense manager built for Indians who want full control of their money — without giving up their data.

No login. No cloud. No internet needed. Everything stays on your phone.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
💰 TRACK EVERY RUPEE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Add income and expenses in seconds. Choose a category, enter the amount, pick a date — done. Your balance updates instantly. Filter by Today, This Month or All Time to see exactly where your money is going.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
📊 BUDGETS THAT WORK
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Set monthly spending limits for Food, Shopping, Travel or any category. The progress bar fills as you spend. Orange when you are near the limit. Red when you have gone over. Stay on track every month.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
🎯 SAVINGS GOALS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Planning a vacation? Saving for a gadget? Set a target amount and deadline. Add money toward it whenever you can. Finance Tracker tracks your progress automatically and tells you when you will reach your goal.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
🔄 RECURRING TRANSACTIONS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Add your salary, rent, EMI and subscriptions once. Finance Tracker automatically adds them on the correct date every time you open the app. No more forgetting your fixed monthly entries.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
💡 SMART INSIGHTS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
See your savings rate, top spending category, daily average spend and how this month compares to last month. Finance Tracker analyses your actual data and gives you insights that help you spend smarter.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
👥 FAMILY MEMBERS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Track finances for your whole family. Each member has completely separate transactions, budgets and goals. Switch between members in one tap from the home screen.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
📤 EXPORT YOUR DATA
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Export your transactions as Excel, PDF, CSV or JSON. Share with your accountant, save for your records or import into any other app. Your data always belongs to you.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
🔒 PRIVATE & SECURE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Finance Tracker never connects to any server. All your data is stored only on your device. Set a 4-digit PIN or use fingerprint to lock the app. We collect zero data about you — read our privacy policy anytime inside the app.
