Privacy Policy for Finance Tracker

Last updated: May 2, 2026

Summary: Finance Tracker stores all your data locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal or financial data on external servers. Your privacy is 100% protected.

1. Information We Collect

Finance Tracker does NOT collect any personal information. The app operates entirely offline and stores data only on your device.

2. How We Use Your Information

All financial data you enter (expenses, income, EMI, investments) is stored locally on your device using browser local storage technology. This data:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is not transmitted to any server
  • Is not accessible to us or any third party
  • Remains under your complete control

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored using your browser's local storage mechanism. This means:

  • Data persists on your device even when offline
  • Data can be cleared by clearing your browser data/cache
  • Data is specific to your device and browser
  • Uninstalling the app will remove all data

4. Third-Party Services

Finance Tracker does not use any third-party services, analytics, tracking, or advertising. The app is completely standalone.

5. Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device and never transmitted, the security of your data depends on your device's security. We recommend:

  • Using device lock screens (PIN, password, biometric)
  • Keeping your device software updated
  • Not sharing your device with untrusted individuals

6. Children's Privacy

Finance Tracker does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13. The app is safe for all ages.

7. Your Rights

Since we don't collect any data, there is no data for you to request, modify, or delete from our servers. You have complete control over your data on your device at all times.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.

9. Data Backup

It is your responsibility to back up your financial data if desired. The app does not provide cloud backup. Consider:

  • Taking screenshots of important records
  • Exporting data periodically (if feature is available)
  • Not clearing browser data unless you want to reset the app
