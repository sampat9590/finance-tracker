Privacy Policy

Finance Tracker — Last updated: May 2026

🔐 Zero Data Collection — Your data never leaves your device
Summary:

Finance Tracker stores all your data locally on your device only. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal or financial data anywhere. Your privacy is 100% guaranteed.

1. Information We Collect

Finance Tracker collects absolutely no personal information. The app operates entirely offline and stores data only on your device using your browser's local storage.

2. How Your Data is Stored

All financial data you enter (expenses, income, budgets, goals, recurring transactions) is stored locally on your device. This data:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is not transmitted to any server
  • Is not accessible to us or any third party — ever
  • Remains under your complete control at all times

3. Third-Party Services

Finance Tracker uses the following external libraries loaded from CDN for app functionality:

  • Chart.js — for analytics charts
  • SheetJS (xlsx) — for Excel export
  • jsPDF — for PDF export

These libraries do not collect any user data. No analytics, no tracking, no advertising services are used.

4. Data Security

Since all data is stored locally and never transmitted, your data security depends on your device. We recommend using device lock screens and keeping your software updated.

5. Children's Privacy

Finance Tracker does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13. The app is safe for all ages.

6. Your Rights

Since we collect no data, there is nothing to request, modify or delete from any server. You have 100% control over your data on your device at all times. Use the "Clear All Data" option in the app to delete everything.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. Changes will be posted here with an updated date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.
