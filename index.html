Privacy Policy
How we handle your data
›
🔒
Your data never leaves your device. No servers. No cloud. No tracking.
What We Collect
Nothing. All data is stored only on your device using localStorage. We do not collect names, financial data, usage stats or any personal information.
How Data Is Stored
All data stays on your device only. No internet required. Uninstalling removes everything. Nothing is ever transmitted to any server.
PIN & Security
Your PIN is stored locally and never transmitted. Biometric authentication uses your device's built-in security only.
Third-Party Libraries
Chart.js, SheetJS and jsPDF are loaded from CDN. No ads, analytics or tracking SDKs are used.
